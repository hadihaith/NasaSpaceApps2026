# Member 3 Earth data checklist

Hadi Cylane · Earth data · 14 November 2026

## What the game needs from you

In **The Great Homecoming**, the lab uses documented historical baselines to initialize hypothetical experiments. The Earth Archive lets players investigate actual Earth records and file what they found. You own the source records, processing, quality checks, and provenance for both uses. Member 1 owns how data reach the game, and Member 4 owns analysis methods and dataset-specific review. ## 2. Resolve units, baselines, and coverage for launch

- [ ] Give Member 4 an aggregation table for each shipping variable and product. Record whether a monthly value is a mean, a total, a rate, or an index, along with its native and output units and the chosen annual interpretation. POWER `PRECTOTCORR` starts as `mm/day`, and IMERG precipitation starts as `mm/hr`. Phoenix outputs convert each to a monthly total in `mm/month`. For annual accumulation, use complete-year sums and `mm/year` if agreed. For rate analyses, agree on day weighting or an equivalent unit conversion. Keep an equal-month mean labeled explicitly if that is what the app shows.
- [ ] Flag the January–September 2025 IMERG endpoint, missing station years, grid-versus-station differences, and source-specific quality limits. For comparisons, identify matching months or full years and document the spatial footprint. The documented overlap for complete IMERG/MERRA-2 years is 1998–2024. Keep the station’s urban, point-scale interpretation visible if it is used.
- [ ] Reconcile the lab’s baseline with the baseline file. The 1991–2020 MERRA-2 normal is already generated. The current Homecoming lab uses all loaded months through 2020, so Phoenix adds 1981–1990. Give Member 1 the agreed baseline period, monthly units and aggregation, sample counts, and intended use for each game variable. Provide the exact periods for any record-specific exceptions.
- [ ] If Member 1 intends to ship the other five Homecoming locations as evidence, inspect the separate `regional-climate.json` together. The Sahel, Amazon, eastern Mediterranean, South Asia, and eastern Australia records identify NASA POWER point requests for 1991–2025. Record their actual coordinates and grid footprint, product/version, query and parameters, retrieval time, units, raw checksums, and how to reproduce the JSON. Flag the point catalog’s `mm/day` rainfall units. Give Member 1 the list of locations and variables ready for the archive. [Homecoming catalog](../public/homecoming/regional-climate.json)

## 3. Close source and quality checks

- [ ] For each release source, give Member 1 the public product citation, required acknowledgement, access requirements, verified redistribution terms, and any limits or unresolved facts. NASA POWER and MERRA-2 need their acknowledgements. IMERG needs its product DOI and the Giovanni acknowledgement. NOAA GSOM redistribution wording is recorded as `UNKNOWN` in the data register. Confirm the terms for the release package or agree on which station data can be deferred. Track unresolved terms as `UNKNOWN` and keep Earthdata tokens and account details out of git and client code.
- [ ] Rebuild from pinned inputs where needed with `python3 scripts/process_data.py build`, then run `python3 scripts/process_data.py validate`. Use the documented account-backed Giovanni export when refreshing IMERG and update the checksum and retrieval record deliberately. Validate the exact release export in addition to the source collection.
- [ ] Deliver a compact per-series quality report with period, cadence, missing count, longest gaps, spatial coverage, units, conversion, known flags, and excluded data. Check calendar continuity, leap years, missing versus true zero, and the number of complete years available to the archive. Carry the existing cross-checks forward. Make clear that monthly agreement between sources can reflect shared seasonality and does not establish a matching trend.
- [ ] Keep the AppEEARS vegetation request as follow-up unless the team reopens scope. If a result is ready and approved for release, document MOD13A3 V061, apply its scaling, fill-value and quality rules, add reproducible area aggregation, and give the new series to Member 4 for review. Preserve model-derived soil wetness and simulated vegetation as their own labeled quantities.
