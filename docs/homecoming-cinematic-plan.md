# Operation: The Great Homecoming — cinematic plan

Open on the AUK astronaut and Space Apps Kuwait marks against black. Let the Apollo picture fill the frame before the CRT bezel reveals who’s watching. Make the party cannon the thread through the story: Cadet B fires it in 1969, a streamer jams it, and all three crew members recognize the same streamer when Zorvax reaches for the trigger at the hearing. The balloon gets the last word.

Unity’s 47.2-second prologue is the proposed timing master, with a new 3.2-second opening card. The browser currently runs 39.3 seconds before recruitment. The proposed shared cut reaches recruitment at **0:50.4**, followed by a **6.4-second lab briefing** and, after gameplay, a **10.2-second hearing and epilogue beat**. The storyboard, hearing variants, and demo timings are proposals. The source assets, story data, and existing captures were inspected in a read-only planning pass. The runtime checks below are for implementation. ## Skills for implementation

- **Blender Animation and Stop Slop are already available locally.** Use Blender Animation to block anticipation, contact, held poses, and follow-through, then check exported GLBs in both engines. The editable rigs document Blender 4.5, so check any 5.x skill examples against the authoring version. Use Stop Slop on original pickups and captions. Drop a line when the prop or eyeline already says it. [glTF Blender exporter](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Blender-IO), [Stop Slop source](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop)
- **Game Feel is the best candidate for a later install.** Its event-timed feedback fits the cannon kick, switch contact, and control response. The listing showed 16.8K installs and 8.9K stars for the source repo. Apply its easing and settling guidance inside the existing shot clocks. The same pack’s **Unity Animation** skill is a lower priority because it focuses on Animator Controllers and humanoid IK, while this film samples imported clips by shot time.
