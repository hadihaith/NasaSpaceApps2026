## The two-minute judge route

| Time | What the judge does and sees |
|---|---|
| **0:00–0:15** | Select **Play Demo**. A short excerpt moves from the AUK and Space Apps Kuwait card through the 3D Moon, Apollo CRT pullback, and 57-year match cut. **Skip to investigation** is visible immediately. |
| **0:15–0:32** | Follow Nyxen to one weather control and run a short lab preview labeled **Simulated scenario**. Then open the rainfall case and call **rising**, **falling**, or **little change** for each location. |
| **0:32–1:12** | Reveal both locations on the KU Exchange Earth. Show the 2024–2025 Sahel dip, then pull back to 1991–2025. Give both charts common axes and a shared year cursor, with a table and direct access to the paired difference. |
| **1:12–1:46** | Offer **Keep my call** or **Revise my call**, then **File finding**. Both choices get the same report-stamp animation. The report shows the variable, the two locations, the rates, and which finding carries the significance test. |
| **1:46–2:00** | Submit to the hearing. Hold the **Historical record** label while Nyxen points to the page and Zorvax lowers his trigger hand. Finish on the balloon and offer **Try Phoenix** or **Play full story**. |

These timings are rehearsal targets. The player can pause and take longer at every decision. Keep the story team’s proposed **50.4-second opening** in the full campaign and use a short excerpt for judging. Put the recommendation to use `/demo` behind a successful clean-session test of the public route.

**Freeze these values for the demo and recording.** Rainfall is NASA POWER corrected MERRA‑2 precipitation at two grid points, **14°N, 6°E** and **36°S, 72°W**. Each annual point is the mean of 12 monthly totals. Over 1991–2025, the descriptive Theil–Sen rates are **+2.13** for the Sahel and **−18.84** for central Chile, in **mm/month per decade**. The paired series is Sahel minus Chile for each matched year. Its Theil–Sen rate is **+24.65**. Its reviewed OLS estimate is **+22.84**, with a **95% interval of +16.36 to +29.33** and **p = 3.23 × 10⁻⁸**. Apply the significance result only to the paired OLS test. Phoenix is the second case, with **45 complete years**, a descriptive **+0.509 °C per decade**, and reviewed **p = 2.14 × 10⁻⁹**. [Evidence and review](../docs/homecoming-evidence.md) · [NASA POWER monthly data](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/monthly/)
