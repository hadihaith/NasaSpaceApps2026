# Member 1 integration checklist

## The game you’re shipping

**Operation: The Great Homecoming** is a browser game for NASA Space Apps’ **Be An Earth System Trend Detective!** challenge. Aliens who love humans mistake Apollo 11 for the beginning of humanity’s departure from Earth. Fifty-seven years later, they recruit the player to run a hypothetical Earth simulator. The player tries combinations of environmental controls, advances simulated time, and collects discoveries in any order. An Earth Archive investigation then asks what changed in an actual historical record, where, by how much, and whether the data support a significance claim. The player files the evidence in a report, watches the final hearing, and can keep experimenting. The intended tone is capable scientists, ridiculous management, and affectionate comedy. [Shared project discussion](https://chatgpt.com/share/6af81532-81d8-83ed-ac56-ecaea838b885) · [NASA challenge objectives](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/earth-system-trend-detective/)

The shared discussion proposed a Vite app. The current project uses Next.js App Router and TypeScript for the game, React Three Fiber for the globe, and Vite for the standalone globe preview. The local Homecoming flow requires three discoveries and one filed Earth investigation for the final hearing. [App manifest](../package.json) · [Game flow](../src/homecoming/Homecoming.tsx) · [Simulation model](../src/homecoming/model.ts)
